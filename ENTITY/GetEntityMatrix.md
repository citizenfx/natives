## GET_ENTITY_MATRIX

```c
// 0xECB2FC7235A7D137 0xEB9EB001
void GET_ENTITY_MATRIX(Entity entity, Vector3* forwardVector, Vector3* rightVector, Vector3* upVector, Vector3* position);
```

Gets the entity's forward, right and up vectors, and its position.

## Parameters
* **entity**: The entity to get the matrix from.
* **forwardVector**: The entity's forward vector.
* **rightVector**: The entity's right vector.
* **upVector**: The entity's up vector.
* **position**: The entity's position.
